Treasure Hunt

The Treasure Hunt project was completed as part of CS370 and involved reinforcement learning and Q-learning. This project gave me experience working with an algorithm that learns from decisions, rewards, and penalties.

Project Information

Course: CS370 Reinforcement Learning

Category: Algorithms & Data Structures

Project: Treasure Hunt

Main Concept: Q-learning and reinforcement learning

About the Project

The Treasure Hunt project was different from some of the other programming projects I completed because the goal was for an agent to learn how to make decisions instead of having every decision directly programmed.

The project helped me understand how reinforcement learning uses rewards and penalties to help an agent improve its decisions over time. It also introduced me to the idea of balancing exploration and exploitation.

My Enhancement

For my CS499 enhancement, I improved the way the training process could be tracked and reviewed. I added additional training information so I could see more of what was happening during the learning process.

The enhanced version tracks information such as the number of steps taken during episodes, exploration and exploitation information, epsilon information, and average steps. I also added training history so that the results from the training process could be reviewed.

What I Learned

One of the biggest things I learned from this enhancement was that being able to see information about an algorithm makes it easier to understand what the algorithm is doing.

Before making the enhancement, it was harder to see what was happening during each episode of training. Tracking additional information gave me a better way to see how the training was progressing.

I also learned more about exploration and exploitation. The agent needs to try different choices while also using choices that it has already learned are useful. Finding a balance between these two ideas is an important part of reinforcement learning.

Challenges

One of the challenges I faced with this project was understanding reinforcement learning and getting the training process to work correctly. I also experienced errors while working with the training environment.

Working through these problems helped me become more comfortable with troubleshooting. Instead of assuming that the entire program was wrong, I learned to look at the error and work through the problem to find where the issue was happening.
